# Code of Conduct

DocuStamp wants to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect that people have different views and limited time.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other place where you represent the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately by email to **conduct@docustamp.dev**. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who does so.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
